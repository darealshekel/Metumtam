Planning information and estimates

Orders come from a fixed MapleScouter reference snapshot calculated for synthetic level 286 profiles targeting approximately 82,500 Boss 380 Normal Power. These assumed profiles are not representative equipment builds. They are adjusted to the levels you enter, not optimized for your character's damage. Skills, costs, translations, and availability can differ by region and patch. Forecasts use your entered income and are estimates, not promises of a completion date. Check current in-game information before spending resources.

Enhance and other planner controls only change the plan in your browser. They do not access or upgrade your game account. You are responsible for your in-game decisions and for keeping any records you need.
