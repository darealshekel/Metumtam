Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Your plan stays in your browser

metumtam does not require an account. Class selections, node levels, goals, material balances, and forecast settings are saved using your browser's local storage under the key hexa-planner-v1. The application does not send your saved plan to a metumtam backend. Imported plan files are read in your browser.

This data remains until you clear it, your browser removes it, or you use a different browser or device. To delete all saved plans, clear this site's storage in your browser settings. That may also remove data for other pages on the same GitHub Pages origin. Anyone who can access your browser profile may be able to view your saved plans.

Hosting and fonts

GitHub Pages delivers the website and may process request information such as IP addresses and browser or device details under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve those requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The application includes no advertising or analytics scripts. Skill descriptions and artwork are bundled with the site, so viewing a skill does not request its description from the wiki.

External links and contact

Following a wiki, source, or repository link takes you to a third-party service with its own privacy practices. If you choose to post on the project issue tracker, your post and GitHub profile information may be public. Do not post sensitive information. No issue is submitted automatically by this planner.

Changes and questions

This notice will be updated when the site's practices change. For questions about the application, contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. For information processed by GitHub or Google, consult their linked policies and contact options.
